Input and offline details

Microphone audio stays on this device and is never recorded or uploaded. Startup calibration measures quiet-room and played-note levels, the piano's harmonic profile, and how three familiar chords sound through this microphone. It saves only compact measurements on this device. The app requests reduced echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic gain control where supported. After the first visit, the practice page is cached for offline use; microphone access requires HTTPS.
