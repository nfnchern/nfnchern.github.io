Connect your piano · Input and offline details

Place your phone near the piano speaker and play clearly, or connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard in a browser that supports Web MIDI. Audio and MIDI input are analyzed on this device and are never recorded or uploaded. Calibration measures the quiet-room level, tunes the microphone input, and learns note spectra from prompted single notes. It saves only compact measurements on this device. The app requests reduced echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic gain control where supported. After the first visit, the practice page is cached for offline use; microphone and MIDI access require a secure context.
